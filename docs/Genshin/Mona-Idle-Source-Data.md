## Historical partial-layout investigation

The following records how the earlier unknown spans were investigated. Its
CurveSpawn attribution, unread header/tail descriptions and alignment interpretation
are superseded by the native transfer proof linked above. Preserve this history
as rejected hypotheses, not current field or readiness claims.

The installed game's `GenshinImpact.exe` (SHA-256 `08A3086D5F3FE695F01DAB61EFA42E442006B18E5E475B2520DF356F6A073B7D`) contains native serializer strings. A bounded read found `ShapeModule.m_CurveSpawn.value`, `.spread`, `.speed.scalar`, and `.speed.minScalar`, as well as `m_DefaultCurveNormal` and `OriginalPoint`. In every Idle and InFloor particle, the 68-byte source extension immediately after the typed Shape is identical. Its first 12 bytes are `(1f,1f,1f)`; the following 56 bytes match the 2017 `MultiModeParameter` shape exactly: `value=0`, `mode=0`, `spread=0`, a speed MinMaxCurve with constant 1 and empty curves. `m_CurveSpawn` is the strongest **candidate** for those 56 bytes because of the native field strings and exact structure. The first vector's field identity and the extension's runtime activation for Shape types 2 and 4 remain unproved. The exporter retains the entire original span and does not silently apply it.

The 52-byte addition after Emission is likewise identical across seven Idle/InFloor particles and contains ten nonzero 32-bit words, including floats `6,3,.4,.6,.8,1,50,150`. A read-only scan of 5,574 same-size source ParticleSystems in `06860440.blk` found the Idle Shape-extension pattern in 5,462, so it is a common default pattern. Among those, the 52-byte block varies independently: words 5–8 are frequently `(1,1,1,1)` but are `(.4,.6,.8,1)` in Idle, with several other ordered float quartets. That resembles a color, but its field name, color space, and runtime effect remain unproved. Installed-player strings expose the standard Emission fields but no extra named `EmissionModule.*` field. It may be another inserted module; assigning names from numbers would be speculation. The 4-byte particle header word is 1, the following 8-byte pair is `(1,20f)`, and the 440-byte tail has 53 nonzero words per particle, with child-specific color-like values. Their runtime roles are unproved. The Collision 4-byte and Trail 380-byte additions are inside **disabled** modules for all five Idle systems, so they do not gate this target's enabled modules.

The installed player's native **property-binding** string references around code file offsets `28623923–28625776` and `28630724–28632795` list, in the same order twice, `m_UseCustomVertexStreams`, `m_EnableGPUInstancing`, `m_EnableGPUInstancingV2`, `m_UseOctagonShape`, `m_OctagonCoordinate` array, `m_RotateWithParent`, `m_ParentScale`, `m_ParentRotation`, `m_VertexStreams`, four `m_Mesh` pointers, `m_MaskInteraction`, and `m_Flip`. These are field-name bindings, **not a recovered serializer/type tree**. Combined with exact source byte widths, alignments, and adjacent independently typed fields, they establish a bounded renderer mapping: byte 212 is `m_UseCustomVertexStreams`; 213–215 are zero alignment padding in **all 41,512** same-size renderers checked; 216–218 are the three listed booleans; 219 is zero padding; 220–287 are eight octagon-coordinate pairs; 288 is `m_RotateWithParent`, 289–291 zero padding; 292–303 are `m_ParentScale` float3; 304–319 are `m_ParentRotation` float4; 320–331 hold stream count/data; 332–379 hold four mesh pointers; 380–383 are mask interaction; 384–395 are `m_Flip` float3. Bubble/LittleSpark have GPU instancing flags `(true,false)` and Drops/Splash `(false,false)`; all five have `useOctagonShape=true`, `rotateWithParent=true`, `parentScale=(1,1,1)`, `parentRotation=(0,0,0,1)`, `flip=(0,0,0)`. The decoder rejects non-boolean values or nonzero alignment bytes, exports these named values, and leaves no renderer-specific unresolved byte span. `m_AllowRoll` and `m_ApplyActiveColorSpace` do not occur in this installed player's binding strings, so the 216 flags do not support using either as a billboard-tilt correction. Byte 212 and byte 380 were independently located against the pinned [Unity 2017.4.30f1 type tree](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AssetRipper/TypeTreeDumps/20ba06e00e0625de0e49a502c6868d6cc86d0f0b/InfoJson/2017.4.30f1.json), SHA-256 `38D77013DE60C22F7883EA1822B94666C40EEF4F83C444D1E40CAD8AE67394BE`.
